Bullet Hell Galaxy Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Who we are

Bullet Hell Galaxy is a single-player arcade game played on your device. This policy describes how the Android app handles information. It is not legal advice.

The game does not create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or photos.

Information stored on your device

The game saves the following information locally with Android SharedPreferences: game settings such as sound, music volume, and difficulty; best score, best survival time, best stage, and total runs; star shards, owned ships, the selected ship, upgrades, and build presets; achievements, daily objectives, sector progress, medals, and boss records.

Saved information restores your settings, high scores, and hangar progress the next time you open the game.

Sharing

Saved scores, ships, and settings stay on this device. The game does not sell that progress. Banner and full-screen advertising is handled by Google AdMob, which receives the advertising data described below.

Android backup

The app allows Android backup. When backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the local save data in the backup tied to your Google account, under Google's own terms.

Advertising

A banner from Google AdMob is shown along the bottom of the screen. While the game is open, that banner changes to another AdMob placement about every 45 seconds. When a run ends, in defeat or victory, a full-screen AdMob ad may appear. AdMob provides the close button on that ad.

Google may collect and use the advertising ID, device information, IP address, and ad interactions under Google's privacy policy. You can reset the advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings. The game does not send saved scores, ships, or settings to AdMob.

Your choices

Open Settings and choose Reset Saved Progress to clear scores, shards, ships, upgrades, and achievements stored by the game. Uninstalling the app removes the local save from the device. You can also turn Android backup off in your device settings.

Children

Bullet Hell Galaxy is a general-audience arcade game and is not directed to children. It does not ask players to create an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will appear in the game and on this page with a new effective date.
